# Diyar-e-Libas homepage benchmark

27 November 2026 · 1366px desktop and 390px mobile · Pakistan storefronts where available

The ten sites below are a relevance-based shortlist. A verified worldwide ranking or comparable sales and traffic data for this specific category was unavailable. SHA LIBAS and Dynasty are closest to the fabric proposition. Cambridge is an adjacent menswear benchmark. The broader retailers provide merchandising and service-navigation references. We inspected each homepage in Chromium at both widths, sampled the page at successive scroll positions and checked footer links. Campaigns can change by region and date. Sapphire did not expose first-screen or footer elements in the mobile sample, and Dynasty did not expose mobile footer links. Their desktop render and available homepage content supplied those observations.

## Final structure

1. **Announcement and navigation.** One delivery offer. Brand mark, three fabric routes and WhatsApp help. Phone navigation fits within the viewport.
2. **Single editorial hero.** Unstitched menswear headline and one shopping CTA on the left. Editorial model photo on the right, with a dark gradient behind the copy across one canvas. Fluid type and crop keep the message readable at phone widths.
3. **Shop by fabric.** Three photo-led cards with the same image aspect ratio. Names, prices and shade links align across one row. Mobile omits secondary descriptions to give shopping information room.
4. **Buying assurances.** Pakistan delivery, the two-suit offer and cash on delivery terms. Verified catalog terms only.
5. **Shopping help.** Compact FAQs and a WhatsApp link for fabric and shade questions.
6. **Footer.** Group shopping links and order help under clear headings.

The primary shopping path is hero CTA → collection cards → shade selection on the existing product pages. Campaign imagery illustrates finished styling. Catalog photography shows the fabrics for sale. The design needs no carousel or JavaScript to render the buying path. JavaScript retains the copyright year. Conversion effects are unmeasured. Collection clicks and completed orders are the metrics to compare when analytics are available.
